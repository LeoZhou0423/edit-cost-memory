Table 2.  The erase trade-off.  Two knobs are swept: the erase scale c, and the erase direction.
With g_j = a_j . e_j, the alignment of the erase direction with the address it targets, and
L1_j the locality at alignment 1, the prediction is residual = |1 - g_j| and locality =
at alignment 1, the prediction is residual = |1 - g_j| and locality = g_j L1_j / g0_j,
where g0 is the alignment of the unscaled direction.  Eliminating the scale gives a V, not a
line: locality = (L1_j/g0_j)(1 - residual) while g_j <= 1, and (L1_j/g0_j)(1 + residual)
past the fold.  m = 64, additive write, erase scaled along the address.
